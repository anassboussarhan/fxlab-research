(Memo1)

Outright purchase and sale operations of JGBs

The Bank of Japan offered outright purchase operations of JGBs worth 300.0 billion yen on March 2, 300.0 billion yen on March 9, 300.0 billion yen on March 16 and 300.0 billion yen on March 27. The purchases were exercised at 300.6 billion yen on March 7, 307.9 billion yen on March 14, 290.8 billion yen on March 22 and 300.3 billion yen on March 30, respectively.

(Memo2)

Securities lending as a secondary source of JGSs

No purchases in March.
