(Memo1)

Outright purchase and sale operations of JGBs
The Bank of Japan offered outright purchase operations of JGBs worth 300.0 billion yen on December 1, 300.0 billion yen on December 8,
300.0 billion yen on December 11 and 350.0 billion yen on December 24. The purchases were exercised at 300.2 billion yen on December 4, 301.8 billion yen on December 11, 300.0 billion yen on December 16 and 341.1 billion yen on December 29, respectively.

(Memo2)

Securities lending as a secondary source of JGSs

The Bank of Japan offered securities lendings as a secondary source of JGSs under repurchase agreements are as follows,
38.2 billion yen on December 18 for 20-year JGB #86

The Bank sold securities under repurchase agreements are as follows,
20-year JGB #86 worth 1.4 billion yen on December 18
