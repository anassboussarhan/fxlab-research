(Memo1)

Outright purchase and sale operations of JGBs
The Bank of Japan offered outright purchase operations of JGBs worth 300.0 billion yen on November 6, 300.0 billion yen on November 10,
300.0 billion yen on November 19 and 300.0 billion yen on November 25. The purchases were exercised at 300.6 billion yen on November 11, 286.5 billion yen on November 13, 300.0 billion yen on November 25 and 300.2 billion yen on November 28, respectively.

(Memo2)

Securities lending as a secondary source of JGSs

The Bank of Japan offered securities lendings as a secondary source of JGSs under repurchase agreements are as follows,
20.3 billion yen on November 21 for 20-year JGB #96

The Bank sold securities under repurchase agreements are as follows,
20-year JGB #96 worth 3.0 billion yen on November 21
